- **Residual delta:** The plausible residual is count-interval propagation and non-vacuity under missing pose, not pose repair, robustness, masking, or point counting.
- **Disposition:** `SELECT_PRIMARY_SOURCE_NOVELTY_CHECK_PROVISIONAL_PREFERRED`. Numeric estimate is 3 GPU-hours and within the idea-creator pilot ceiling; no implementation authority follows.

### 2. Fragment-Boundary Transport for Count-Mass Conservation

- **Strongest objection:** Conserving a frozen base response can preserve a biased counter, while endpoint correction across long gaps may be unidentifiable and may reduce to generic tracklet/gap completion.
- **Cheap decisive gate:** Freeze the base response before cuts and compare no correction, linear endpoint correction, and transport on held-out cut/gap families. Kill unless transport halves intact-versus-fragment mass error, keeps wrong-identity boundary weight at or below 10%, and leaves byte-identical untouched identities unchanged.
- **Residual delta:** The plausible residual is an explicit compositional law for count mass across supplied-track fragmentation and re-entry, not tracking, temporal consistency, overlap-add, or a new base counter.
- **Disposition:** `SELECT_PRIMARY_SOURCE_NOVELTY_CHECK`. Numeric estimate is 6 GPU-hours and within the idea-creator pilot ceiling; no implementation authority follows.

### 3. Leave-One-Person-Out Common-Mode Cancellation

- **Residual delta:** A task-specific nuisance front end is plausible, but robust common-mode subtraction is classical and MultiCounter+ already owns multi-person consistency.
- **Disposition:** `RETAIN_AS_LOW_COST_NUISANCE_CONTROL_NOT_METHOD_CLAIM`. Numeric estimate is 1 GPU-hour; low cost does not cure the weak novelty residual.
