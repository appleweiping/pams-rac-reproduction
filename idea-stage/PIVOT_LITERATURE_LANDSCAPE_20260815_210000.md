## Theme map

### A1 — pose repair and occlusion-robust point counting

SSTRAC, Sight and Sound, and the MultiCounter family make repair, alternate-modality reliability, skeleton occlusion robustness, and robust point prediction occupied ground. The unresolved task-level question is narrower: can missing-pose ambiguity be propagated into a useful repetition-count set without confusing self-consistency with true-count coverage?

### A2 — pose/motion hypothesis sets and conformal task uncertainty

CHAMP, CUPS, uncertainty-aware pose forecasting, latent conformal motion forecasting, MotionMap, and recent safety-UQ work make multi-hypothesis pose/motion prediction and generic conformalization crowded. A defensible residual, if one survives full-text adjudication, must be the count functional and a non-vacuity/coverage protocol—not “set-valued UQ,” “pose uncertainty,” or “occlusion robustness.”

### B1 — track fragmentation, re-entry, association, and repair

GTA, STAR, TrackTrack, labeled-RFS tracking, GTATrack, SRITrack, Split and Connect, and probabilistic tracklet inpainting cover the generic split/link/re-entry/gap-repair landscape. In a supplied-track setting, tracking and re-identification are explicitly not available contribution claims.

### B2 — density integration, additivity, and temporal consistency

RepNet, TransRAC, MultiCounter, Motion Feature Learning, Rethinking TSSM, PAMS, and TWCRAC occupy response integration, density prediction, self-similarity reconstruction, action-start response, and local/multi-scale cycle consistency. People-flow work shows that conservation-based counting is also established in an adjacent domain. The unresolved question is whether a count-specific boundary term across supplied fragments is more than generic repair plus conservation of a possibly biased response.

## Actual gaps, stated provisionally

1. Direction A: in the checked accessible sources, direct RAC handles occlusion through repair, fusion, robustness, or point prediction, while pose-UQ work returns pose or motion hypotheses/distributions. No checked source directly propagates missing-pose uncertainty to a calibrated, non-vacuous repetition-count interval. This is an unconfirmed landscape gap, not novelty clearance.
2. Direction A protocol caveat: containment of the method’s own unmasked estimate is deterministic self-consistency, not empirical coverage of the true count. Any later claim would need a target, exchangeability/shift assumptions, post-freeze label coverage, interval width, abstention rate, and non-vacuity controls.
3. Direction B: track repair/re-entry and RAC response integration/temporal consistency are both crowded. No checked accessible source explicitly learns an additive repetition-count-mass composition law with a boundary correction across supplied identity fragments and abstention when a join is unidentifiable. This remains unconfirmed.
4. Direction B risk: reproducing the intact output of a frozen base counter can conserve its bias; long gaps and tempo changes can make boundary correction unidentifiable.
5. Direction B claim boundary: the residual cannot be tracking, re-identification, generic endpoint matching, temporal consistency, density integration, or a new base periodic representation.
