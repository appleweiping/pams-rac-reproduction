 491     5. Conclusion and Limitations
 492     We specify a pose-driven MRAC counter for people whose
 493     pace changes within a track. The central design separates
 494     identity state while sharing parameters, routes overlapping
 495     windows from local period evidence, fuses continuous re-
 496     sponses by normalized overlap-add, and decodes each track
 497     once. The setting does not remove all annotation and does
 498     not jointly optimize perception and counting: the counting
 499     objective is intended to avoid person-wise count and period
 500     labels, while upstream perception may use external supervi-
 501     sion.
 502        This draft has three material limitations. The current
 503     local checkout contains only a single-person PAMS repro-
 504     duction; the multi-person data path, router, continuity loss,
 505     and boundary decoder await synchronization with collabo-
 506     rator code. No eligible MultiRep artifact has been received,
 507     so the manuscript makes no performance claim. Finally,
 508     a modular pose/track pipeline inherits missed detections
 509     and identity switches and may scale with the number of
 510     active people. The final study must quantify each limita-
 511     tion through oracle/predicted tracks, corruption analysis, and
 512     population-scaling measurements. Until those gates close,
 513     the paper remains provisional and data-pending. MultiRep is
 514     synthetic, and single-person transfer cannot establish multi-
