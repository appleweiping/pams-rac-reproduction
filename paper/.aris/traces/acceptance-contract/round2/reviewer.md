# Fresh Acceptance-Contract Review — Round 2

Route: fresh zero-context, same-family reviewer; adversarial validator check

Verdict: **REVISE**.

The conceptual assertions are appropriate, but eleven gates were not yet
executable enough to support submission assurance:

1. AC01 needed a real delivery-receipt/manifest/clean-HEAD checker.
2. AC02 needed canonical story, title, abstract, notation, equation-order, and
   one-to-one contribution/experiment-map checks rather than substring tests.
3. AC05 needed a frozen collaborator commit, source resolution, real hash
   verification, enums, order checks, and one-PASS-anchor/all-PASS-test closure.
4. AC07 needed a structured nine-scope count-and-period label firewall.
5. AC09 needed mandatory hash-bound PDF coverage, exact denial contexts, and
   manifest-resolved comparative claims.
6. AC10 needed executable figure-triplet/XML/caption/visual-review checks and
   one consistent PPT evidence class.
7. AC11 needed a non-circular receipt/manifest design and byte-opening evidence
   precheck.
8. AC11--AC12 needed semantic method/protocol/frontend/family/binding checks.
9. AC13 needed recomputed means, sample SD, run/seed pairing, and verified
   video-cluster bootstrap intervals.
10. AC15 needed PDF binary/metadata and hash-bound extracted-text coverage plus
    exactly one anonymous declaration.
11. AC16 needed one fail-closed aggregator that actually invokes every gate and
    checks freeze, freshness, template, build, anonymity, and reviewer health.

These are implementation requirements, not requests to weaken or waive any
blocked scientific evidence. Round 3 must inspect the executable revisions.
