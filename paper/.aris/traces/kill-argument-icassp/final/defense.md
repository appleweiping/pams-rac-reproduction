# ARIS Kill Argument - Independent Defense and Adjudication

## Final verdict

**KILL SURVIVES FOR THE CURRENT SCIENTIFIC ARTIFACT. STRONG REJECT IF SUBMITTED NOW.**

The defense succeeds on a narrower and important point: this is an unusually candid, internally consistent, evidence-gated **pre-results specification**, not a deceptive or padded empirical paper. It clearly marks the proposed system as unimplemented, withholds every result-dependent statement and value, separates incompatible protocols, and makes submission mode fail when evidence or synchronization fields are missing. The attack therefore does not establish fabrication, hidden evidence, or conceptual impossibility.

It does establish present scientific insufficiency. The frozen method manifest has no implementation commit or source anchors for the proposed system; the results manifest contains no eligible experiment; and the paper supplies neither a theorem nor an executable training and inference specification that could establish the central robustness hypothesis. Those three P1 findings remain unresolved. Candor protects the integrity of the pre-results package, but it cannot make the current PDF scientifically acceptable.

This adjudication is final for the frozen artifact named below, while its independence label remains **same-family / provisional** because the attack and defense reviewers are from the same model family.

In submission mode, missing results, protocol fields, result claims, abstract results, pending fields, and sync-required interfaces raise hard LaTeX errors. ## Adjudication standard

## P1 - Fatal now

### P1.1 Proposed method absent - `still_unresolved`

**Defense.** The paper never represents the tracked single-person checkout as the proposed system. The introduction, figure caption, experiment section, conclusion, and method manifest all state the mismatch. The manifest's claim ceiling explicitly prevents the single-person dominant-pose counter from evidencing the multi-person routing method. This is excellent provenance control and defeats any suggestion that the manuscript silently relabels an existing implementation.

**Adjudication.** The factual core remains: the proposed implementation commit is null; all eight central components, task contract, training objective, and fallback have no source anchors or tests. A publishable empirical method must exist as a frozen scientific object. Disclosure does not supply it.

### P1.2 No eligible feasibility, performance, robustness, or mechanism evidence - `still_unresolved`

**Defense.** The artifact prevents unsupported evidence from leaking into prose or tables. Every empirical field is visibly pending, the abstract result is withheld, the diagnostic contains no fake trace, the results manifest is table-ineligible, and all evidence gates are false. This is exactly correct behavior for a pre-results package.

**Adjudication.** There is still no eligible pilot, count metric, robustness result, routing trace, boundary test, uncertainty estimate, or failure analysis. The central hypothesis has neither empirical support nor a formal proof. If evaluated as a scientific submission now, this independently requires rejection.

### P1.3 Mathematical specification non-executable - `still_unresolved`

**Defense.** The equations do fix several nontrivial semantics: windows never combine identities; masks enter the local evidence; router weights lie on a simplex; experts emit responses rather than rounded window counts; responses are aggregated before one decoder call; and uncovered samples are excluded. The manuscript explicitly labels the remaining choices as proposal-level interfaces rather than pretending the equations are complete code.

**Adjudication.** The projection to the scalar signal, period/confidence map, support and fallback, router and expert architecture, targets and gradient paths, taper, gaps, response calibration, event decoder, and actual objective remain unspecified. These choices determine the learned function and output. Multiple incompatible algorithms satisfy the displayed equations, so the current artifact is not executable or reproducible.

## P2 - Fundamental scientific blockers

### P2.1 Person semantics under supplied and predicted tracks - `partially_answered`

**Defense.** The claim is expressly conditional on supplied persistent identities. The paper says that predicted indices are current tracklets, enumerates fragmentation, re-entry, switches, retirement, and reconciliation, and disables predicted-track counting rows until those semantics and the assignment to ground-truth people are source-bound. Oracle and predicted protocols are not conflated. Conditioning a counting method on tracks is scientifically legitimate; a counting paper need not also solve detection and tracking.

**Remaining issue.** The lifecycle contract is absent, so predicted outputs are not yet stable person-wise estimands. Under supplied persistent tracks, the method processes identity streams independently with shared parameters; evidence is still needed to show that the contribution is more than applying a local single-track counter to each supplied identity. The attack is narrowed, but not closed.

### P2.2 Novelty as a composition claim - `partially_answered`

**Defense.** The paper does not claim ACF, FFT, irregular-sampling analysis, overlap-add, or mixture-of-experts individually. It states a specific systems hypothesis: window-local tempo evidence, soft response routing, aggregate-before-decode, and identity-local state for within-track pace changes. A new ordering and problem-specific integration can be a valid signal-processing contribution if it produces a non-obvious, controlled benefit; novelty does not require every primitive to be new.

**Remaining issue.** With the router, objective, and decoder unbound and no comparison against the closest period-adaptive systems, the artifact cannot yet show a distinct technical principle or a non-obvious interaction. The novelty case is plausible at proposal level but unproven scientifically.

### P2.3 Supervision boundary - `partially_answered`

**Defense.** The actual text is carefully qualified: only the intended counting objective is said to omit person-wise count and period labels. External pose and tracking supervision is disclosed, and the required firewall explicitly covers loaders, pseudo-targets, router targets, auxiliary losses, tuning, selection, stopping, calibration, inference, perception, tracking, and evaluation. The claim cannot be enabled before a single-commit audit.

**Remaining issue.** The implementation and gradient paths do not exist in the frozen evidence, so the end-to-end supervision property cannot yet be verified. The wording and firewall answer overclaiming, but not the scientific property itself.

## P3 - Validity and falsifiability

### P3.1 Core hypothesis lacks a fully frozen estimand - `partially_answered`

**Defense.** The manuscript defines more of the intended estimand than the attack's shorthand suggests: a paired clean-versus-piecewise-warped comparison that preserves identity and source-event count, separates occlusion and identity-switch stress, records routing and reconstructed responses, freezes selection or reports an aggregate, and pairs the synthetic test with held-out natural nonstationarity. It also names the counting metrics and paired video-cluster uncertainty.

**Remaining issue.** No primary endpoint, tempo-severity distribution, effect threshold, stationary equivalence/noninferiority margin, natural-cohort rule, or final generator is frozen. Those choices can change the conclusion. The hypothesis is testable in outline, not yet preregistered tightly enough for a result claim.

### P3.2 Displayed ablation does not isolate local tempo routing - `still_unresolved`

**Defense.** The plan includes ACF-only, FFT-only, single-, hard-, and uniform-routing variants; parameter/context/frontend/training matching; paired videos and seeds; collapse checks; boundary-offset controls; and a separate track-global baseline. These choices show awareness of mechanism identification.

**Adjudication.** The displayed global row changes both tempo locality and routing from soft to uniform, while the router and experts can use the full representation and ignore explicit period evidence. The track-global baseline is not defined enough to supply the missing soft-global counterfactual. Cue blocking/shuffling and capacity-matched non-routing controls are absent. Favorable values in the current table would not uniquely identify local tempo routing as the cause.

### P3.3 Protocol fairness - `partially_answered`

**Defense.** Protocol separation is one of the draft's strongest features. Native/contextual systems, supplied oracle tracks, and common predicted tracks are divided and never ranked across protocol boundaries. A baseline enters a matched block only if input, output, tracking, and evaluation parity can be demonstrated; otherwise its values are contextual or not applicable. The manifest must bind release, split, frontend, evaluation unit, assignment, aggregation, and evaluator hash.

**Remaining issue.** None of those matched systems or bindings currently exists. Adapting video systems to pose tracks may change the methods materially, and Track-PAMS and track-global-tempo lack source-bound definitions. The fairness policy is credible, but the actual comparison is not yet instantiated.

### P3.4 Reconstruction guarantee is overstated - `answered`

**Defense and adjudication.** The attack attributes a stronger guarantee than the manuscript makes. Equation (4) defines one aggregate response followed by one decoder invocation. The text claims only that this order avoids independently decoding every overlapping window and therefore removes that specific structural route to duplicate counts. It immediately says that the operation does **not** guarantee one peak, requires timestamp alignment and positive coverage, leaves taper and decoder semantics source-bound, and plans a boundary-offset test. Averaging inconsistent responses can still duplicate, erase, or shift peaks, but the manuscript does not deny that. The limited topology claim is valid; empirical boundary safety remains withheld under P1.2 rather than being falsely guaranteed here.

## P4 - Material methodological weaknesses

### P4.1 Scalar local-tempo identifiability - `partially_answered`

**Defense.** The paper itself flags periodic visibility, out-of-range masking, minimum lag-pair support, low-support fallback, pair-dependent centering or another irregular-sampling estimator, and periodic/nonperiodic missingness controls. It also avoids claiming that the current ACF/FFT cue is already reliable.

**Remaining issue.** The scalar projection, window/cycle requirements, lag and frequency ranges, treatment of joint-specific confidence, and half/double-period behavior are not fixed or tested. The proposed controls are appropriate but cannot establish identifiability before execution.

### P4.2 Statistical plan incomplete - `partially_answered`

**Defense.** At least three named seeds, mean and sample standard deviation, paired video-cluster 95% bootstrap intervals, paired videos, and pre-result freezing of primary outcome, contrast direction, replicate count, multiplicity, and exclusion rules form a serious pre-results scaffold. Published checkpoints are correctly barred from supporting variance claims.

**Remaining issue.** The scaffold intentionally leaves the primary endpoint, bootstrap count, multiplicity policy, failures/exclusions, sample-size rationale, natural cohort, and stationary-preservation margin unset. It is not yet a complete preregistration.

## P6 - Precision and terminology

### P6.1 Person, persistent identity track, and current tracklet - `partially_answered`

The manuscript explicitly distinguishes supplied persistent identities from predicted tracklets and conditions its principal claim accordingly. However, `person i`, `identity`, and `tracklet` still share closely related output notation across protocols. The final paper needs protocol-specific evaluation-unit terminology, especially if reconciliation changes the mapping from tracklets to people.

### P6.2 Undefined persistent `state` - `still_unresolved`

Figure 1 labels `STATE s_i` and the prose refers to identity-indexed state, but Equation (1) defines a window encoder without a state type, initialization, update, retention, or retirement rule. The intended meaning may be buffers and responses, but the frozen artifact does not define it.

### P6.3 Undefined fast/medium/slow scales - `partially_answered`

The paper explicitly calls these intended scales rather than observed specializations and requires frozen tempo bands plus collapse and routing controls. That avoids presenting the labels as discovered facts. Units, band boundaries, receptive fields, and fallback still require source binding.

### P6.4 Undefined `reconstruction invariant` - `partially_answered`

Equation (4) and the surrounding text make the intended operational property inferable: identity-separated responses are combined on aligned time coordinates and decoded once rather than counted window by window. The paper also denies a one-peak guarantee. The phrase `reconstruction invariant` itself is never formally stated, however, so it should not carry theorem-like weight until its assumptions and property are defined.

## Does the strongest kill argument survive?

**Yes, for the current artifact.** The shortest sound kill chain is:

1. The central scientific hypothesis concerns a proposed multi-person local-routing system.
2. The method manifest confirms that this system has no frozen implementation, objective, source anchors, or tests; the only tracked executable system cannot evidence it.
3. The results manifest confirms that there is no eligible dataset/protocol binding, run, prediction, metric, uncertainty estimate, or audit.
4. The paper offers no theorem that could replace empirical validation, and its equations intentionally leave decision-bearing interfaces open.

Therefore the current PDF cannot establish that the method exists as claimed, can be trained, is novel in effect, improves within-track tempo robustness, preserves stationary behavior, or attributes any future gain to the proposed mechanism. P3.2 reinforces the kill but is not needed once P1.1-P1.3 stand.

The attack does **not** kill the research direction permanently. A frozen collaborator implementation, source-bound objective and supervision audit, stable evaluation-unit semantics, an unconfounded local-versus-global mechanism test, and eligible MultiRep results could materially change the verdict. Those artifacts are not present here, so their future success cannot be presumed.
