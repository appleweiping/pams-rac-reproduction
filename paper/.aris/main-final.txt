 494     5. Conclusion and Limitations
 495     We specify a pose-driven MRAC counter for people whose
 496     pace changes within a track. The central design separates
 497     identity state while sharing parameters, routes overlapping
 498     windows from local period evidence, fuses continuous re-
 499     sponses by normalized overlap-add, and decodes each track
 500     once. The setting does not remove all annotation and does
 501     not jointly optimize perception and counting: the counting
 502     objective is intended to avoid person-wise count and period
 503     labels, while upstream perception may use external supervi-
 504     sion.
 505        This draft has three material limitations. The current
 506     local checkout contains only a single-person PAMS repro-
 507     duction; the multi-person data path, router, continuity loss,
 508     and boundary decoder await synchronization with collabo-
 509     rator code. No eligible MultiRep artifact has been received,
 510     so the manuscript makes no performance claim. Finally,
 511     a modular pose/track pipeline inherits missed detections
 512     and identity switches and may scale with the number of
