\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We formulated multi-person repetition counting as inference on masked,
identity-indexed pose signals whose tempo can change within a track. The
proposed specification estimates local periodic evidence, softly routes shared
response experts, reconstructs each identity response with normalized
overlap-add, and decodes that response once. This pre-results draft has no
local multi-person implementation or eligible MultiRep results, and its pose
and tracking frontends rely on external supervision. Completion requires a
frozen collaborator commit, source-bound losses and interfaces, matched oracle
and common-track evaluation, and paired piecewise-tempo diagnostics. Empirical
and citation audits must then be rerun before any quantitative or comparative
conclusion is enabled.
